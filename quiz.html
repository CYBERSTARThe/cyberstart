Cybersecurity Level 2

Test your understanding of cybersecurity concepts. Choose the best answer for each question.

QUESTION 1 OF 10

A company gives employees access only to the files required for their jobs. Which security principle does this demonstrate?

Least privilege means giving users only the access they need to perform their responsibilities.
QUESTION 2 OF 10

Which part of the CIA Triad focuses on preventing unauthorized people from accessing information?

Confidentiality focuses on ensuring information is accessible only to authorized people or systems.
QUESTION 3 OF 10

What is the primary difference between authentication and authorization?

Authentication answers "Who are you?" Authorization answers "What are you allowed to access?"
QUESTION 4 OF 10

Why is multi-factor authentication generally stronger than using only a password?

MFA adds another verification factor, so knowing the password alone may not be enough to access the account.
QUESTION 5 OF 10

What is the main purpose of hashing a password?

Password systems commonly store a derived representation rather than the original password.
QUESTION 6 OF 10

An attacker sends a fake message pretending to be a company's IT department and asks an employee to provide login information. What type of attack is this?

Social engineering attacks manipulate people into performing actions or revealing information.
QUESTION 7 OF 10

Which security approach uses multiple layers of protection instead of relying on a single defense?

Defense in depth uses multiple security controls so one failed defense does not necessarily expose the entire system.
QUESTION 8 OF 10

Which CIA Triad principle focuses on keeping systems and information accessible when they are needed?

Availability means authorized users should be able to access systems and information when needed.
QUESTION 9 OF 10

A security team discovers that a software application has a weakness that could be exploited by attackers. What is this weakness commonly called?

A vulnerability is a weakness in a system that could potentially be exploited to compromise security.
QUESTION 10 OF 10

After detecting a cybersecurity incident, why is documenting what happened important?

Documentation helps security teams understand what occurred and improve future detection and response.
