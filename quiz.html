🧠 Cybersecurity Quiz

Test your cybersecurity knowledge. Choose the best answer for each question and learn from the explanations.

QUESTION 1 OF 10

What does MFA stand for?

Correct answer: A

MFA stands for Multi-Factor Authentication. It requires additional verification beyond just a password.
QUESTION 2 OF 10

What is phishing?

Correct answer: B

Phishing is a social engineering technique that attempts to trick people into revealing information or performing an unsafe action.
QUESTION 3 OF 10

What does a firewall help control?

Correct answer: B

A firewall helps control network traffic according to security rules.
QUESTION 4 OF 10

What is malware?

Correct answer: A

Malware is software designed to perform harmful or unwanted actions.
QUESTION 5 OF 10

What should you do with a suspicious link?

Correct answer: C

Suspicious links should be verified through a trusted source before interacting with them.
QUESTION 6 OF 10

Which security principle gives users only the access they need?

Correct answer: B

Least privilege means giving users only the access necessary to perform their responsibilities.
QUESTION 7 OF 10

Which part of the CIA Triad focuses on protecting information from unauthorized access?

Correct answer: A

Confidentiality focuses on making sure information is accessible only to authorized people or systems.
QUESTION 8 OF 10

What does encryption do?

Correct answer: B

Encryption transforms readable information into protected data that requires the appropriate key or process to understand.
QUESTION 9 OF 10

What is a vulnerability?

Correct answer: B

A vulnerability is a weakness in a system that could potentially be exploited.
QUESTION 10 OF 10

What is social engineering?

Correct answer: B

Social engineering involves manipulating people into revealing information or performing actions.
