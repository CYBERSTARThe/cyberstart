Cybersecurity Lessons

Learn the fundamentals of cybersecurity one lesson at a time.

Lesson 1

Introduction to Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, accounts, systems, and information from unauthorized access and digital threats.

Cybersecurity is important because people and organizations store large amounts of information digitally.

Lesson 2

Phishing and Scams

Phishing is a type of social engineering attack designed to trick someone into revealing information or taking an unsafe action.

Suspicious messages may contain unusual links, unexpected attachments, urgent requests, or requests for sensitive information.

Lesson 3

Passwords and Account Security

Strong, unique passwords can help protect online accounts. Using the same password for many accounts can increase the impact if that password is exposed.

Multi-factor authentication adds another layer of protection by requiring an additional verification step.

Lesson 4

Malware

Malware is software created to perform harmful or unwanted actions on a computer or other device.

Different types of malware can behave in different ways, which is why keeping software updated and being careful with downloads is important.

Lesson 5

Firewalls and Network Security

A firewall helps control network traffic based on security rules. It can help prevent unauthorized network connections from reaching protected systems.

Firewalls are one part of a larger security strategy.
