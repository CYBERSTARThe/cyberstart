Common Cyber Threats

🦠 Malware

Malware is software designed to damage systems, steal information, or perform unwanted actions.

🎣 Phishing

Attackers may use fake emails, messages, or websites to trick people into giving away information.

🧠 Social Engineering

Social engineering uses manipulation and deception to convince people to reveal information or take an action.

Stay Safe Online

🔑 Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords and avoid reusing the same password across multiple accounts.

⚠️ Watch for Scams

Be careful with unexpected links, attachments, messages, and requests for personal information.

🔄 Keep Software Updated

Software updates often include security fixes that help protect your devices.

Cybersecurity Careers

🛡️ Security Analyst

Security analysts monitor systems and networks for suspicious activity and help protect organizations from cyber threats.

🔎 Penetration Tester

Penetration testers legally test systems for security weaknesses so organizations can fix them before attackers find them.

🚨 Incident Responder

Incident responders help investigate and contain cybersecurity incidents when something goes wrong.
