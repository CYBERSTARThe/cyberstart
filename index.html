Cybersecurity Basics

Welcome to CyberStart! This website is designed to help beginners understand the basics of cybersecurity.

What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, accounts, and information from digital threats.

What Is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of scam where someone tries to trick you into giving away information or clicking a dangerous link.

What Is Malware?

Malware is software designed to harm a computer, steal information, or perform unwanted actions.

Strong Passwords

Strong passwords are difficult to guess. Using a combination of letters, numbers, and symbols can make an account harder to break into.

What Is a Firewall?

A firewall helps control network traffic and can block unwanted connections from reaching a device or network.

Stay Safe Online

Think carefully before clicking links, downloading files, or sharing personal information online.
