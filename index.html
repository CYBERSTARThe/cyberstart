What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, phones, networks, accounts, and information from digital threats.

Why Is Cybersecurity Important?

Cybersecurity helps protect people and organizations from threats such as stolen information, hacked accounts, scams, and malicious software.

Common Cybersecurity Threats

Phishing: Attempts to trick people into revealing information or clicking dangerous links.

Malware: Harmful software that can damage systems, steal information, or perform unwanted actions.

Password attacks: Attempts to gain access to accounts by guessing or obtaining passwords.

Scams: Deceptive messages or websites designed to trick people.

How Can You Stay Safer Online?

Use strong, unique passwords: Avoid using the same password for every account.

Turn on multi-factor authentication: MFA adds another verification step when signing in.

Keep your software updated: Updates often include important security fixes.

Learn to recognize phishing: Be careful with unexpected messages asking you to click links, download files, or provide personal information.

Be careful with downloads: Only download software and files from sources you trust.

Cybersecurity Is Everyone's Responsibility

You don't have to be a cybersecurity expert to practice good security. Small habits can make a big difference when staying safe online.
