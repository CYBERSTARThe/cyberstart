What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, accounts, and information from digital threats.

What Is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of scam where someone tries to trick you into giving away information or clicking something dangerous.

What Is a Firewall?

A firewall helps control network traffic entering or leaving a computer or network.

What Is Malware?

Malware is software designed to cause harm, steal information, or perform unwanted actions.
