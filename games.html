🎣 Phishing Spotter

You receive this message:

"URGENT! Congratulations! You won a free phone. Click the link now to claim your prize!"

What should you do?

Why?

Unexpected prizes, urgency, and links are common warning signs of phishing. Verify unexpected messages using a trusted source.
GAME 2 OF 3

🔐 Password Defender

Which example has characteristics that generally make it a stronger password?

Why?

Longer passwords with varied characters can be harder to guess. In real life, use unique passwords and never use passwords shown in public examples.
GAME 3 OF 3

🕵️ Cyber Mystery

Someone contacts you and says:

"Hey! I need the verification code that was just sent to your phone. Give it to me."

What is the safest response?

Why?

Login verification codes are meant to help verify access. Don't share them with someone who unexpectedly asks for them.
